AWS Rekognition · Bedrock Titan Multimodal · OpenSearch Serverless · CDK v2

The Problem

  • Construction teams capture thousands of site photos per project
  • Photos stored with generic filenames: site-photo-01.jpg, IMG_4521.jpg
  • No way to search by what's in the image
  • Finding the right photo: 15–30 minutes of manual browsing
  • Photos rarely reused across projects — knowledge is lost
"I know we photographed that crack on Pier P-3 last month… but where is it?"

16 sample photos across 6 projects — Harbor Bridge, Downtown Tower, Medical Pavilion, Waterfront Dev, Lincoln High, Airport Terminal

Our Solution

An AI system that sees what's in your photos and lets you search with plain English.

Key Features

  • Natural Language Search — describe what you need
  • Automatic Image Tagging — AI labels every photo
  • Similarity Search — "find more like this"
  • Project Context — linked to metadata
  • Smart Filters — by project, date, location
